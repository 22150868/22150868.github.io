Micro sim The micro sim is a smaller version of the standard sim with it only being 15x12mm in size. They were created to be used in the earlier versions of the smartphone such as the iphone 4 meaning you will not find them in today's version of mobile devices
Nano sim The Nano sim is the smallest sim card out there with it being only 12.3x8.8mm in size. It was made to be small to take up less space.
