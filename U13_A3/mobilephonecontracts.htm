Mobile phone contracts

A mobile phone contract is a fixed term agreement between you and a provider which usually lasts between 12 to 36 months and pays a monthly fee for things such as data messages and calls. Mobile phone contracts bundle the cost of a physical phone with a cheaper overall price that you pay each month for a set period of time. They can also offer upgrades for your phone for later models and better hardware. They also allow you to spread payments out rather than pay it all in one go which can be ideal for some people who may not have enough money to pay it all on hand.
